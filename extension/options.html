About FormPilot

A careful autofiller for repeat form details.

Local by default

Your profile and learned corrections stay in this browser's extension storage. FormPilot does not upload them to a FormPilot server.

You stay in control

FormPilot fills only supported matches after you start a fill. Ambiguous fields, existing answers, sensitive fields, and agreements are left for you to review.

  • FormPilot never submits a form.
  • FormPilot does not overwrite answers you already entered.
  • Export or delete your locally stored data in Privacy & Data.
