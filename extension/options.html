About FormPilot

A local-first form autofiller for Edge and Firefox.

Hi, I'm Divyanshu — a college student in India. I built FormPilot because Google Forms honestly irritated me. Not that they're bad or anything; I just got form fatigue filling in the same old details again and again. I'm also an Edge main (yeah, we exist), and there was no reliable way to keep my info stored and fill repeated stuff faster.

I hope this extension helps you. It's free, and it always will be. If you like my work, consider helping this poor dev. Bye 💜
